The problem

Post-raise is where ownership operations usually break down.

Closing a round is the beginning of a long administration relationship with your investors. Most companies are not prepared for what comes after: calculating who is owed what, handling transfer requests, managing exit events, and keeping investor records current.

Distribution calculations done manually
Revenue-share, profit-share, and exit entitlements calculated outside the platform — prone to errors and inconsistency.
Transfer requests handled through email
No structured process for reviewing, approving, or restricting investor transfers — creating risk and administrative drag.
Corporate events disrupt the ownership record
New rounds, buybacks, restructures, and exit events require ownership record updates that are hard to track without a connected system.
What Arcstone covers

The full ownership lifecycle, from close to exit.

Distribution entitlement tracking

Revenue-share, profit-share, dividend-like, and exit-based entitlements tracked at the investor level — connected to participation terms in the ownership record.

Transfer restrictions and controls

Eligibility-aware transfer controls applied consistently — so participation rights move only when the conditions defined in the ownership record are met.

Corporate events

New funding rounds, buybacks, restructures, and other corporate events are recorded and reflected in the ownership record — keeping the cap table current over time.

Investor updates and reporting

Structured updates, distribution notices, and investor communications sent from within the platform — with records connected to each investor's profile.

Exit administration

When an exit event occurs, entitlement calculations, investor records, and ownership positions are already structured and ready for distribution processing.

Governance and resolutions

Board resolutions, shareholder resolutions, and class consents move through configurable approval thresholds with notice periods — from draft through counsel review to adoption. Nothing gets adopted by email thread.
