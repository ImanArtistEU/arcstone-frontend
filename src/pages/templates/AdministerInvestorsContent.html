The problem

Investor administration is scattered across tools, inboxes, and folders.

After a raise, companies are left managing a growing base of investors using a combination of email, spreadsheets, shared drives, and manual processes. Every update, every document request, every transfer gets handled differently — and records drift out of sync.

Before Arcstone
Investor data in emails. Agreements in folders. Onboarding in forms. Updates sent manually. No single source of truth.
With Arcstone
Every investor connected to their records, documents, rights, and ownership position — in one structured environment.
How it works

One connected investor workflow.

1

Invite and collect

Investors receive a secure, structured onboarding link. They submit their identity information, contact details, and any required documents — all captured in a consistent format.

2

Verify eligibility

Identity and eligibility checks are completed before investors access participation terms or execute agreements. KYC/AML workflows can be applied where required.

3

Execute agreements

Participation agreements and supporting documents are signed and stored inside the platform — directly connected to the investor's record and the ownership structure.

4

Add to the ownership record

Once onboarding is complete, the investor is added to the live cap table with their participation terms, rights, and eligibility status attached to their record.

5

Manage and communicate over time

Investor updates, distribution notices, document requests, and lifecycle communications are managed from the same environment — so records stay current and nothing gets lost in email.

A portal built for each audience

Every stakeholder sees exactly what they need.

Investors, employees, and professional advisors each get their own live, read-only portal assembled directly from the ownership record: holdings, transactions, rights, and documents for investors; vesting explained in plain language for employees; scoped, time-limited access for professionals such as auditors or counsel.

Every view and download is logged. Matched to a single profile whether the investor first showed up as a fundraising contact or a cap table holder, so a CRM relationship and a shareholder record never drift apart.
