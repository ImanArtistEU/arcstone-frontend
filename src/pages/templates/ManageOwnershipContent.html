The problem

Spreadsheets break as ownership gets more complex.

Most private companies start with a spreadsheet cap table. That works at the beginning. By the second or third round, the spreadsheet is outdated, fragmented, and impossible to audit. Documents live in different folders. Rights are in separate agreements. No one has a single complete picture.

Cap table in a spreadsheet, last updated 3 months ago
Investor agreements stored across email threads and shared drives
Rights and participation terms not connected to ownership records
No audit trail when ownership events happen — rounds, transfers, restructures
What Arcstone provides

Everything ownership requires, in one structured record.

Live cap table

A structured record of every shareholder, share class, round, transfer, and ownership change — updated in real time, not batch-synced from a spreadsheet.

Rights and participation terms

Share classes, economic participation rights, transfer restrictions, and liquidation preferences connected to the ownership record — not buried in separate documents.

Investor records linked

Each investor's identity, verification status, participation details, and agreements are connected to their position in the cap table — not stored separately.

Ownership event history

Every round, transfer, restructure, and corporate event is recorded with a complete and reviewable history — so the ownership record is always auditable.

Transfer and restriction controls

Ownership movements are managed inside the platform, with transfer restrictions, eligibility checks, and company-defined controls applied consistently.

SAFEs, notes, and warrants

Tracked as structured, calculable instruments — not static line items. Conversion terms are computed automatically and reflected in the cap table.

Live readiness score

A live score highlights exactly what to fix before a raise or audit — missing KYC, undocumented convertible terms, grants with no vesting schedule, and more.
