Our mission

We sit at the intersection of private-market fundraising, legal-tech, and compliance automation.
Our mission is to build a serious fintech infrastructure platform that helps startups and SMEs raise capital through structured, non-voting participation rights, preserving founder control and modernizing investor administration.

Core Principles

-A

Founder-friendly control

We believe founders should be able to raise capital or reward contributors without turning every participant into a shareholder with voting power or veto rights.
-B

Contract-first, always

The contract creates the enforceable right. Arcstone's platform represents, tracks, and manages that right against the ownership record, so administration never drifts from what was legally agreed.
-C

Compliance by design

Our platform is built to support compliant administration. Transfer controls, KYC, and eligibility checks are integrated into how every instrument is managed, not layered on afterward.
-D

End-to-end administration

We are not just a document generator. We provide a complete lifecycle layer — from legal document generation to cap table management, governance, and automated distributions.
A serious infrastructure provider, not a speculation platform.
We are building a platform designed around signed legal agreements, verified identities, and authoritative registers. We do not support lawless finance or speculative culture.

The ownership gap in private markets

Publicly listed companies have access to sophisticated, regulated infrastructure for managing shareholders, issuing instruments, and administering ownership events. Private companies do not.
The result is fragmented, error-prone ownership management that creates real risk for founders, investors, and the companies themselves. Cap tables on spreadsheets. Share certificates in email threads. Investor records that no one fully trusts.
“Every company deserves the same quality of ownership infrastructure that was previously only available to those with a stock exchange listing.”

that infrastructure.

What we are building toward

Universal ownership access

A world where any private company — at any stage — can manage its ownership with the same rigour as a publicly traded one. No lawyers required to look up who owns what.

Structured private markets

Ownership instruments that are digital and rules-based by default — with compliance, transferability, and participation rights defined at the instrument level, not managed after the fact.

Founder control by default

Infrastructure that lets founders raise from many investors without giving up the governance control that makes great companies possible. Economic participation without governance compromise.

Arcstone today
We are building the ownership infrastructure layer that private companies have always needed: integrated and designed for the complexity real companies face at every stage of growth.
That means cap tables, investor workflows, governance, and lifecycle administration in one connected system — where every record is current, every obligation is tracked, and every stakeholder always knows exactly where they stand.
