The problem

Traditional fundraising creates problems that outlast the raise.

Raising from investors is not just a financial transaction. It is the beginning of an ongoing ownership relationship. Most companies are not set up to manage that relationship well.

Governance complexity
Traditional equity rounds give investors governance rights and board influence, creating a power dynamic that grows with every round.
Cap table debt
Too many investors, poorly documented rights, and fragmented ownership records make future rounds, exits, and administration harder.
Post-raise administration
Most platforms help you close a round. Few help you manage what comes after: investor records, updates, distributions, and rights.
Never start from a blank list

Find the right funding before you ask for it.

Most fundraising starts with a spreadsheet of names copied from someone else's list. Arcstone scores and ranks live funding opportunities — funds, accelerators, grants, and competitions — against the company's own stage, sector, geography, and traction, and surfaces what's missing before an application goes out.

Non-dilutive routes are flagged alongside equity ones, so raising doesn't default to giving something up.

Arcstone's approach

Structured participation rights. Ownership stays clean.

Economic participation, not governance rights

Investors participate in the company's economic upside through structured rights — without receiving voting shares or board influence unless you choose to give them.

Investor onboarding built in

Collect investor information, run eligibility checks, execute participation agreements, and add investors to the ownership record — all from one workflow.

Cap table connected from day one

Every investor, round, and participation right is connected to a live, structured ownership record — not a separate spreadsheet or document folder.

Documentation and agreements

Participation agreements, investor rights documents, and supporting terms are managed inside the platform and connected to each investor record.

A secure data room for diligence

Share diligence materials with per-recipient access and every view logged. Investors get exactly what they need and nothing more.

Post-raise administration included

After the raise, Arcstone supports distributions, investor updates, transfer requests, and lifecycle events — so the ownership record stays accurate over time.
